# Privacy and data

## How patient data is protected
BookMyDoctor stores patient information securely and follows HIPAA rules for health data in the United States. Data is encrypted when sent over the internet and when stored. Only the patient's treating doctors and authorized staff can see medical records.

## Who can see chat messages
Messages sent to the Vaidya Mitra assistant are stored in the patient's account. Doctors do not read the assistant chat unless the patient shares it. BookMyDoctor staff may review chats only to investigate a complaint or a technical problem.

## Sharing medical records
A patient can download their visit summaries and test reports from the app and share them with another doctor. BookMyDoctor never sells patient data and only shares records with insurers for claims the patient has made, or when required by law.

## Marketing emails
BookMyDoctor sends appointment reminders and receipts by default. Marketing and health-tip emails are sent only if the patient opts in, and the patient can unsubscribe at any time from the email footer or the Profile page.
