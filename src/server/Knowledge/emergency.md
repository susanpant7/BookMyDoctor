# Emergencies

## BookMyDoctor is not an emergency service
BookMyDoctor is for booking regular appointments. It is not an emergency service, and the chat assistant cannot give emergency medical help. In a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room right away. Do not wait for an appointment.

## Emergency warning signs
Call 911 for: chest pain or pressure, severe difficulty breathing, signs of stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech), fainting or unconsciousness, severe bleeding that does not stop, a seizure, a severe allergic reaction with swelling of the face or throat, a sudden very severe headache, a serious head injury, poisoning or overdose, or thoughts of suicide or self-harm.

## Signs of stroke (BE FAST)
Use BE FAST to spot a stroke: Balance loss, Eyes (sudden vision loss), Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call 911. A stroke is an emergency; call 911 immediately and note the time the symptoms started.

## Mental health crisis
If you or someone else is thinking about suicide or self-harm, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (available 24/7 in the US), or call 911. BookMyDoctor appointments are not suitable for a mental health crisis.

## Poison emergencies
For a suspected poisoning or medicine overdose, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 (US, 24/7) or call 911 if the person is unconscious, having trouble breathing, or having a seizure.

## Urgent but not life-threatening
For problems that need care today but are not life-threatening, such as a high fever, a painful ear infection, or a minor cut, patients can try a same-day appointment in General Practice (adults) or Pediatrics (children). If no same-day slot is available, visit an urgent care center.
