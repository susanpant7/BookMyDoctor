# Preparing for your visit

## What to bring to an appointment
For any appointment, bring a photo ID, your insurance card (if using insurance), a list of all current medicines with doses, any recent test reports, and your booking reference number (starts with "BMD-"). Arrive 10 minutes early to check in at reception.

## First visit at BookMyDoctor
On a first visit, the patient fills in a short health form at reception or in the app beforehand, covering medical history, allergies, current medicines, and emergency contact. Filling it in the app saves about 10 minutes at the hospital.

## Fasting before blood tests
Some blood tests need fasting. For a lipid profile (cholesterol) or fasting blood sugar test, do not eat for 9 to 12 hours before the test; plain water is allowed. Take regular medicines as usual unless the doctor says otherwise. Book these tests for the morning so fasting is easier.

## Bringing a companion
Patients may bring one adult companion into the consultation room. Children under 18 must attend with a parent or legal guardian. Interpreters can be arranged if requested at least 48 hours before the visit.

## Masks and illness at the hospital
Patients with a cough, cold, or fever are asked to wear a mask in the hospital waiting area. Free masks are available at reception. Patients with symptoms of a contagious illness may be asked to wait in a separate area.

## Medical certificates and reports
Doctors can provide a medical certificate (sick note) for work or school if needed after the consultation. Visit summaries and test reports are uploaded to the patient's account within 48 hours.
