# Account and app help

## Creating an account
To create a BookMyDoctor account, open the app, choose "Need an account?" on the sign-in page, and enter your name, email address, and a password. An account is required to book appointments and to chat with the Vaidya Mitra assistant.

## Signing in and staying signed in
Patients sign in with their email and password. For security, a sign-in session expires after a while; after that, the patient must sign in again. If the app shows an error after being open for a long time, signing in again usually fixes it.

## Forgotten password
If a patient forgets their password, they can choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the link sent to their email to set a new one. The reset link expires after 30 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder.

## Updating profile details
Patients can update their phone number, address, insurance details, preferred pharmacy, and emergency contact from the Profile page. The email address on the account cannot be changed; contact support to move an account to a new email.

## About the Vaidya Mitra assistant
Vaidya Mitra is the BookMyDoctor chat assistant. Vaidya Mitra can explain which doctor to see for a symptom, share information about doctors, fees, the hospital, and policies, and help patients book appointments. Vaidya Mitra is an AI assistant, not a doctor, and cannot diagnose illnesses or prescribe medicine.

## Chat history
Messages with the Vaidya Mitra assistant are saved to the patient's account and are visible on any device where the patient is signed in. Opening the chat in two browser tabs shows the same conversation in both.

## Contacting support
BookMyDoctor support can be reached by email at support@bookmydoctor.example or by phone at (800) 555-0199, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Central Time. Support can help with account problems, payments, refunds, and complaints.

## Deleting an account
Patients can ask to delete their account by contacting support. Upcoming appointments must be cancelled first. Medical records are kept for the period required by law even after the account is deleted, but they are no longer accessible through the app.
