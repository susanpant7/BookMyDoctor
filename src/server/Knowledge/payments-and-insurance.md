# Payments and insurance

## Consultation fees
Each doctor has a consultation fee for a new visit and a lower follow-up fee for a visit about the same problem within 30 days. Fees are shown on the doctor's profile and can be looked up by the Vaidya Mitra assistant.

## Accepted payment methods
BookMyDoctor accepts credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), Apple Pay, Google Pay, HSA and FSA cards, and the BookMyDoctor wallet. Cash is accepted at the hospital reception.

## When payment is taken
The consultation fee is paid when the appointment is booked online. Extra charges, such as lab tests, X-rays, or procedures done during the visit, are paid at the hospital after the visit. The hospital confirms the price of any extra test before doing it.

## Using insurance
Patients should add their insurance details in their profile before the visit and bring their insurance card. Not every insurance plan is accepted, and some plans have restrictions; the Vaidya Mitra assistant can check whether a plan is accepted.

## Insurance copay and claims
When a patient uses insurance, they pay only their plan's copay at booking. BookMyDoctor sends the claim to the insurer. If the insurer rejects the claim, the patient is billed the remaining amount and receives an itemized receipt.

## Receipts and invoices
A receipt is available in the app for every paid appointment, under My Appointments. Patients can download an itemized invoice for insurance reimbursement or tax purposes.

## Discounts
Students and seniors over 65 receive a 10% discount on consultation fees when they show a valid ID at the hospital. A second child from the same family booked on the same day in Pediatrics receives 20% off.
