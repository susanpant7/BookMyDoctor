# Symptom guide: which specialty to book

This guide helps patients choose the right specialty. It is not a diagnosis. If any emergency warning sign is present, the patient should call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room instead of booking an appointment.

## Headache and migraine
For a headache or migraine in an adult, book General Practice. For a headache in a child, book Pediatrics. A sudden, very severe headache ("the worst headache of my life"), a headache with confusion, weakness, trouble speaking, a stiff neck with fever, or a headache after a head injury is an emergency: call 911.

## Fever, cold, flu and cough
For fever, cold, flu, runny nose, or cough in an adult, book General Practice. For the same symptoms in a child under 18, book Pediatrics. A fever in a baby younger than 3 months, a fever above 104°F (40°C), or a fever with difficulty breathing needs urgent care: call 911 or go to the emergency room.

## Sore throat and ear pain
For a sore throat or ear pain in an adult, book General Practice. For a sore throat or ear infection in a child, book Pediatrics. BookMyDoctor does not offer ENT; General Practice can refer the patient if needed.

## Stomach pain, nausea, vomiting and diarrhea
For stomach pain, nausea, vomiting, acidity, constipation or diarrhea in an adult, book General Practice. For a child, book Pediatrics. Severe stomach pain that does not go away, vomiting blood, black stools, or signs of dehydration (very little urine, dizziness, dry mouth) need urgent care.

## Chest pain
Chest pain can be an emergency. Chest pain or pressure that is severe, spreads to the arm, jaw or back, or comes with sweating, shortness of breath or fainting: call 911 immediately. For mild chest discomfort that has already been checked and is not an emergency, or for follow-up after a heart test, book Cardiology.

## Palpitations, high blood pressure and heart concerns
For a racing or irregular heartbeat (palpitations), high blood pressure readings at home, high cholesterol, or a family history of heart disease, book Cardiology. Palpitations with fainting, chest pain or severe shortness of breath are an emergency: call 911.

## Shortness of breath
Sudden or severe shortness of breath is an emergency: call 911. Breathlessness that slowly gets worse with activity, especially with swollen ankles, should be seen by Cardiology. Wheezing or asthma in a child should be seen by Pediatrics; in an adult, start with General Practice.

## Skin rash, itching and hives
For a skin rash, itching, hives, eczema, or psoriasis, book Dermatology. For a rash in a child with fever, book Pediatrics. A rash with swelling of the lips, face or tongue, or difficulty breathing is a severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis): call 911.

## Acne, moles, hair and nails
For acne, a mole that has changed in size, shape or color, hair loss, dandruff, or nail problems, book Dermatology. Dermatology also offers yearly skin cancer checks.

## Back pain and neck pain
For back pain or neck pain, book Orthopedics. If the pain started after a fall or accident, or if it comes with numbness in both legs, loss of bladder or bowel control, or fever, seek emergency care immediately.

## Joint pain, knee pain and shoulder pain
For knee, shoulder, hip, wrist, ankle or other joint pain, stiffness, arthritis, or swelling of a joint, book Orthopedics. A joint that is red, hot and swollen with fever needs urgent care.

## Sports injuries, sprains and fractures
For a sprain, strain, sports injury, or follow-up after a fracture, book Orthopedics. If a bone may be broken (deformed limb, cannot bear weight, severe swelling), go to the emergency room first for an X-ray, then follow up with Orthopedics.

## Tiredness, weight changes and general checkups
For constant tiredness, unexplained weight gain or loss, a yearly health checkup, diabetes care, or thyroid problems, book General Practice.

## Urinary problems
For burning while urinating, frequent urination, or a suspected urinary infection in an adult, book General Practice. For a child, book Pediatrics.

## Allergies
For seasonal allergies, sneezing or itchy eyes in an adult, book General Practice. For skin allergies and hives, book Dermatology. For allergies in a child, book Pediatrics.

## Children's health
For any health problem in a baby, child or teenager under 18, book Pediatrics. This includes vaccinations, growth checks, school physicals, and childhood illnesses.

## Mental health, anxiety and depression
BookMyDoctor does not offer psychiatry or psychology. For anxiety, low mood, stress or sleep problems, book General Practice, who can help and refer to a mental health specialist. If someone is thinking about harming themselves, call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) or call 911 immediately.

## Eye, teeth and women's health problems
BookMyDoctor does not offer eye care, dentistry, or gynecology. For these problems, patients can book General Practice for initial advice and a referral to an outside specialist.

## Not sure which specialty
If the patient's symptom is not listed here or the patient is unsure, book General Practice. A general practitioner can examine the patient and refer to the right specialist.
