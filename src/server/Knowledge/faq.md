# Frequently asked questions

## What is BookMyDoctor?
BookMyDoctor is an app for finding and booking appointments with doctors at BookMyDoctor Hospital in Austin, Texas. Patients can browse doctors by specialty, book in-person consultations at the hospital, and chat with the Vaidya Mitra AI assistant for help choosing a doctor.

## How do I find out about a doctor?
Each doctor's specialty, experience, languages, fees and a short bio are shown on the Doctors page. The Vaidya Mitra assistant can also look up this information and check the doctor's available times.

## Can I see a doctor today?
Same-day appointments are sometimes available. The earliest time that can be booked online is 2 hours from now. Ask the Vaidya Mitra assistant to check today's available slots; if none are free and the problem is urgent, visit an urgent care center.

## Can I get a prescription refill?
Prescription refills for ongoing medicines can be requested through a follow-up visit at the hospital. General Practice handles most routine refills, such as blood pressure, diabetes, and thyroid medicines.

## Do you offer vaccinations?
Childhood vaccinations are given in Pediatrics. Adult flu shots are given in General Practice during flu season (September to March) and are free with most insurance plans.

## Do you offer home visits?
BookMyDoctor does not offer home visits or video consultations. All appointments are in person at the hospital.

## Can I choose my doctor's gender or language?
Patients can choose any available doctor in a specialty. Each doctor's languages are listed on their profile. Interpreters for other languages can be arranged with 48 hours' notice, and patients can ask for a chaperone during any examination.

## Can I get a second opinion?
Yes. Patients can book any BookMyDoctor doctor for a second opinion. Bring all previous reports, scans, and prescriptions to the appointment so the doctor can review them.

## Is the Vaidya Mitra assistant a doctor?
No. Vaidya Mitra is an AI assistant. It can suggest which specialty to see, share information about doctors, the hospital and policies, and help book appointments, but it cannot diagnose illnesses or prescribe medicine.
